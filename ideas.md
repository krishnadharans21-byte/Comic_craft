# ComicCraft Design Brief

## Direction exploration

- **Theme Name:** Panel Pop — Warm paper, confident ink, bright cobalt, and tiny coral accents make a comic-creator workspace feel tactile and energetic. **Probability:** 0.04
- **Theme Name:** Midnight Storyboard — Deep plum canvas, electric cyan panel edges, and cinematic gradients evoke a late-night graphic-novel studio. **Probability:** 0.07
- **Theme Name:** Field Notes Atelier — Moss green, parchment, and restrained serif typography frame story creation as a quiet illustrated journal. **Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Panel Pop

- **Design movement:** Contemporary editorial illustration with the clarity and energy of a printed comic zine.
- **Core principles:** Make the five-panel promise immediately legible; reduce the form to an inviting writing prompt plus simple creative controls; celebrate user-created stories rather than crowding the page with dashboard chrome; maintain high contrast and generous readable spacing.
- **Color philosophy:** Warm off-white paper (#FBF7EE), near-black ink (#1E2024), saturated cobalt (#3559E8), energetic coral (#F16D52), pale butter (#F5DF8C), and soft sage (#DCE8D5). Flat color with a small amount of subtle paper texture only where it does not hurt readability.
- **Layout paradigm:** Editorial two-column creator view on wide screens (creative brief on the left, a five-panel illustrated teaser on the right), collapsing to a single column on mobile. Comic preview uses a clear vertical sequence of five paper-like cards with alternating small visual accents. Keep the form and panels in a centered, comfortable-width container.
- **Signature elements:** Thick ink-like borders, offset cobalt/coral corner marks, panel-number tabs, short all-caps section labels, and a compact comic-book wordmark. Avoid complex illustration as UI ornament.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Warm, straightforward, and forgiving. Label every field; show examples as optional hints; validate inline; make generation progress explicit; use disabled/busy states during requests; offer a clear demo mode and a visible explanation when AI services are not configured.
- **Animation:** Brief and purposeful only—subtle hover lift and loading progression. Honor reduced-motion preferences; avoid flashing or distracting looping animation.
- **Typography system:** System-ui sans serif for controls and readable body text, with Georgia or another available traditional serif reserved for the large editorial headline and story captions. Use strong size/weight hierarchy and generous line-height.
- **Brand essence:** “Your idea, frame by frame.” A friendly, capable creative studio that puts the user’s imagination at the center.
- **Brand voice:** Encouraging, concise, story-minded, and jargon-free; explain missing API configuration without implying the app is broken.
- **Wordmark/logo:** ComicCraft wordmark paired with a bold, simple open comic-book/page shape and one cobalt speech-tail/ink slash. Use text in live HTML rather than baked into the icon artwork.
- **Signature brand color:** Cobalt blue (#3559E8), grounded by ink and warm paper.

## Logo production brief

- **Purpose:** A recognizable project icon and browser favicon for ComicCraft.
- **Medium:** Flat square PNG app/logo icon.
- **Subject:** One abstract open comic page with two bold panel blocks and a small speech-tail/ink slash; no text.
- **Composition:** Centered, large, high-contrast silhouette with comfortable background-colored clearance; full-bleed opaque warm-paper background that reaches all edges and corners.
- **Style:** Minimal geometric editorial comic mark, solid cobalt/ink with a coral accent.
- **Constraints:** 1:1 square, opaque, simple filled shapes, readable at 24–48 px. No gradients, no texture, no shadows, no border, no rounded canvas or inset badge, no words, no watermark.
- **Acceptance risks:** Baked-in rounded mask, tiny unreadable detail, illegible generated text, or background padding that does not reach the corners.
